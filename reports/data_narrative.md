# 📖 Data Narrative

## The Story of Our Data: From Acquisition to Loyalty

Our data tells a compelling story of a business that is extremely proficient at acquiring customers but struggles to build initial habits. 

**Chapter 1: The Acquisition Engine**
Our Prophet models show that our marketing campaigns are highly effective. Every time we turn the marketing engine on, we see a predictable and immediate 12% lift in daily revenue. We know how to get people through the door.

**Chapter 2: The Value Trap**
However, getting them through the door is costing us. In regions like South India, we are driving massive volume but our Average Order Value has plummeted. We are acquiring discount-hunters, not brand loyalists. 

**Chapter 3: The Loyalty Cliff**
This discount-heavy strategy reflects in our funnel metrics. 78% of customers never return after their first purchase. But here is the silver lining: if we can get a customer to buy just a *second* time, their probability of becoming a long-term loyal customer skyrockets to 60%.

**The Conclusion**
We must pivot our strategy. We don't need more first-time buyers; we need more second-time buyers. The data clearly dictates that retention, not acquisition, is the lever we must pull for the next 90 days.
